# Mates

Mates are persistent AI collaborators with defined roles, instructions, Knowledge, and private conversations. Use one when you want a continuing working relationship across tasks.

## Start a relationship

Select **+** in the rail (**Add Mate or project**), choose **Create a Mate**, and describe its purpose to Clay. Review the proposal and complete the new Mate's interview. Give examples of the help you want, communication style, and boundaries.

Try a small real request before relying on it for a long workflow. “Review this proposal for unsupported assumptions and ask me one useful question” is an easy way to assess a reviewer Mate.

The [creation and identity guide](/docs/mate-creation-identity) covers the full setup, including name, avatar, color, and reshaping its behavior.

## Maintain the collaborator

Open the Mate's full workspace and select the header chevron labeled **Open Mate settings**. General, Model, and Prompt settings affect different parts of its behavior. Save changes and verify the actual result.

Use [Mate settings and conversations](/docs/mate-settings-conversations) to continue the correct exchange or work in the native conversation. Different conversations share the Mate identity but do not automatically contain every detail of each other.

Use [Knowledge, Memory, and Me](/docs/mate-knowledge-memory-me) to choose the appropriate home for reference material, recorded memories, and owner preferences.

## Do more

[Mate schedules](/docs/mate-schedules) deliver reviewed recurring prompts to a private conversation. [Debates](/docs/debates) bring multiple Mate roles to a decision.

A Mate remains owner-private. Ordinary project sharing or administrator status does not expose its conversation. Connected services still require their own authorization, and ordinary Mates do not receive every Clay's workspace controls.

For project execution, use a project Driver and [Drivers and Workers](/docs/drivers-workers). See [Projects and Mates](/docs/clay-vs-mates) for the boundary between owner-level workspace controls and persistent collaborators.
